   product**, appearing across nearly every branded query and several generic ones. It's currently

   returned other apps' listings and generic tethering-app roundups, not
   `com.alparslan.tetherlinkutility`'s own page.

   `RiFi2k/unlimited-tethering`, `PairVPN`, `NetShare`, `FoxFi`, carrier-official pages). This isn't
   a beatable field yet, it's a not-yet-in-the-field situation.
