Can You Bypass an iPhone's Hotspot Limit?

Published September 23, 2026

Short answer: not the way you can on Android. iOS doesn't let a regular App Store app take over how your iPhone's shared hotspot connection is routed for other devices, Apple's platform rules don't allow that kind of system-level interception. So if you're looking for an iPhone app that does what a tool like Tether Link Utility does on Android, it doesn't exist, and it's not a gap anyone's just failed to fill yet, it's a platform restriction.

What you can actually do if the limit is on your iPhone itself

Your realistic options are the same ones that apply on any carrier, not an iPhone-specific trick:

  • Check if your carrier sells a hotspot add-on for your specific plan, availability and price vary by carrier and tier.
  • See the full comparison of every realistic option (paid add-ons, TTL-based approaches, VPNs, local proxies) in our hotspot bypass guide, most of these apply on iOS too, just with different apps than on Android.

What you can do if it's someone else's hotspot you're connecting to

This is the case Tether Link Utility actually solves today: if there's an Android phone in the picture, either yours or someone else's, running Tether Link Utility as the host, your iPhone can connect to it as a client over a standard SOCKS5 proxy, and that connection isn't treated as separate hotspot usage by the carrier on the Android side.

iPhone is fully supported as a client, just not as the device running the local proxy itself. See the iOS setup steps (works with Shadowrocket or Potatso Lite, both handle the SOCKS5 configuration in a couple of taps).
