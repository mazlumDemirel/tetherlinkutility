Turkcell Hotspot Data Limit Explained

Published September 18, 2026

In Turkey, Turkcell, Vodafone, and Türk Telekom all track tethering (often called "paylaşım kotası" or tethering quota) separately from the main package data, a pattern familiar to anyone who's tethered a laptop only to see it slow down long before their phone's own data ran out.

How the tethering quota works

Turkish operators have historically bundled a specific tethering/paylaşım allowance into postpaid packages, often a fraction of the total package size, after which tethered speeds are reduced for the rest of the billing cycle, even if the phone's own on-device data is far from exhausted. Exact allowances differ by operator and specific tariff, and change over time, check your current package's terms via your operator's app (BiP/Turkcell'im, Vodafone'um, Türk Telekom'um) for the number that applies to you.

How this is detected

The mechanism is the same as everywhere else, Turkish operators infer tethering from the traffic itself (TTL decrement, traffic-pattern/DPI analysis), not from asking permission. Read the full explanation of carrier hotspot detection.

The underlying detection methods (TTL, DPI) aren't specific to Turkish operators, the same techniques show up in how US and Indian carriers detect tethering too, just applied to different plan structures.
