The real problem

Hotspot data caps aren't the same as your data plan.

In the US (including Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile), India (Jio, Airtel, Vi), Türkiye (Turkcell, Vodafone, Türk Telekom), and parts of Europe, many carriers meter or throttle tethered traffic separately from your regular data — even on plans advertised as "unlimited." They detect tethering by how the traffic looks on the wire, not by how much of your plan you've actually used.

Tether Link Utility routes everything through a local proxy on the device itself — so from the carrier's side, it's indistinguishable from normal, single-device usage. No separate hotspot bucket to run out of.

How it works
STEP 01

Tap START SERVER

The Wi-Fi hotspot turns on automatically — no need to dig through system settings first. The app creates a local SOCKS5 proxy tunnel and displays the connection details.

STEP 02

Show the QR code

One QR code carries the Wi-Fi network and the proxy address together. The network name and password are also shown as plain text for laptops.

STEP 03

Connect your device

Android: scan the QR code — on most devices, Wi-Fi joins and the proxy connects automatically. If it doesn't, join the Wi-Fi shown and scan again. Windows / macOS / iOS: join the Wi-Fi shown, then enter the IP and port in your SOCKS5 proxy settings.

STEP 04

Browse freely

Traffic routes through your mobile data. No carrier throttling, no remote server, nothing leaves your hardware.

Features

One-tap tunnel

START launches the proxy immediately. No configuration wizards, no accounts, no cloud dependency.

QR code setup

Tap "Show QR Code" on the server device. On the client Android phone, tap "Scan QR Code · Connect to Server" — on most devices, Wi-Fi joins and the proxy connects automatically, no typing needed.

Fully local

No remote VPN server. No traffic logging. Every packet stays within your own hardware chain.

IP change detection

Carrier IPs shift between sessions. The app detects changes and alerts connected devices automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How does Tether Link Utility bypass hotspot limits?

It runs a local SOCKS5 proxy via Android's VpnService API, making traffic look like single-device usage to carrier DPI systems.

Do I need root access?

No, it works entirely without root privileges.

Does it use a remote server?

No, everything runs locally on your device; no traffic leaves your hardware chain or gets logged.

Ready to share your connection?

Free to install. No account, no cloud server, no setup wizard.
