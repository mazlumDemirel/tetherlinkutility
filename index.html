How it works
STEP 01

Tap START SERVER

The Wi-Fi hotspot turns on automatically, no need to dig through system settings first. The app creates a local SOCKS5 proxy tunnel and displays the connection details.

STEP 02

Show the QR code

One QR code carries the Wi-Fi network and the proxy address together. The network name and password are also shown as plain text for laptops.

STEP 03

Connect your device

Android: scan the QR code, on most devices, Wi-Fi joins and the proxy connects automatically. If it doesn't, join the Wi-Fi shown and scan again. Windows / macOS / iOS: join the Wi-Fi shown, then enter the IP and port in your SOCKS5 proxy settings.

STEP 04

Browse freely

Traffic routes through your mobile data. No carrier throttling, no remote server, nothing leaves your hardware.

Frequently asked questions

How does Tether Link Utility bypass hotspot limits?

It routes traffic shared over your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot (no USB cable involved) through a local SOCKS5 proxy via Android's VpnService API, which helps it avoid some common carrier hotspot restrictions.

Do I need root access?

No, it works entirely without root privileges.

Does it use a remote server?

No, everything runs locally on your device; no traffic leaves your hardware chain or gets logged.

Can I connect a Windows or Mac laptop?

Yes, point your laptop's SOCKS5 proxy settings (built into macOS, or via Firefox/FoxyProxy on Windows) at the IP and port shown in the app. That covers apps which check that setting; for full system-wide coverage including DNS, use sing-box in TUN mode instead.

Does a SOCKS5 proxy setting protect all my traffic?

Not entirely. A browser or system SOCKS5 setting only reroutes traffic from apps that check it, DNS lookups and other background traffic can still leave over the plain hotspot connection unprotected, which some carriers can detect even if your main browsing works fine. For genuinely full coverage on Windows or macOS, run sing-box in TUN mode, which routes your whole system's traffic, DNS included, through the tunnel.

Does it work with iPhone?

iOS has no native SOCKS5 setting, but apps like Shadowrocket or Potatso can connect to the proxy the same way, for that app's own traffic only, not the whole device. A verified full-device option isn't currently available on iOS.

How many devices can connect at once?

The free tier connects 2 devices; Pro raises that to 5 devices at once.

Does it work with 5G and 4G/LTE?

Yes, it shares whatever active mobile data connection your phone currently has, regardless of network generation.

Does Tether Link Utility work with unlimited data plans?

Yes. Many "unlimited" plans still cap or throttle hotspot/tethered traffic specifically, separate from on-device data. Tether Link Utility helps avoid that separate hotspot restriction.

Does my carrier know I'm using tethering?

Carriers typically detect tethering by how traffic looks on the wire, not by asking permission. Routing traffic through a local proxy changes that traffic pattern, which is how Tether Link Utility helps avoid common hotspot restrictions.

Is this a free, unlimited hotspot app?

The free tier connects up to 2 devices at once, with ads, it's free to use, but not literally unlimited in device count. Pro removes ads and raises the limit to 5 devices. Either way, there's no separate hotspot data cap or speed throttling, you're sharing your own mobile data plan.
