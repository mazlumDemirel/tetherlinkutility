# Pricing: Tether Link Utility

## Free
- Price: $0
- Limits: 2 connected devices at once, banner and interstitial ads shown
- Features: Local SOCKS5 proxy for sharing mobile data, no root, no remote server, no account required, works with Windows/macOS/Android/iOS clients

## Pro
- Price: from $2.99/month; exact price shown in the store; varies by country and platform (a yearly plan is also available)
- Limits: 5 connected devices at once, no ads
- Features: Everything in Free, with no ads and up to 5 devices at once

Purchased and managed through Google Play on Android or the App Store on iOS. No web checkout, no custom/enterprise tier currently offered.

The $2.99/month figure is a USD starting point, not a universal price. Actual pricing, including local currency and the yearly plan, is set per country and per platform, so it can differ meaningfully from a direct USD conversion. The exact price is shown in Google Play or the App Store.
